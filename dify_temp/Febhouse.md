# FEBHOUSE - ARCHITECTURE + TECHNOLOGY STUDIO

## 1. About Febhouse & Founder
Febhouse is an independent architecture and technology studio based in Hanoi, Vietnam. We design architectural projects and develop professional SketchUp extensions used by architects worldwide. Our mission is to make architectural design smarter, faster, and more sustainable by bridging the gap between complex spatial ideas and clear visual communication.
* **Founder:** Mr. Nam Nguyen (Nguyễn Hà Nam) — Architect & Software Developer. He is the creator of Sun Diagram, Dynamic Symbols, and Shadow Slice. He also shares tutorials and templates through ArchiDiagram.com.
* **Ecosystem:** The Febhouse ecosystem includes architectural/landscape design, SketchUp Extensions (Sun Diagram, Dynamic Symbols, Shadow Slice), and the educational platform ArchiDiagram.com.
* **Recognition:** Febhouse is an Official Verified SketchUp Extension Vendor on the SketchUp Extension Warehouse.

## 2. Architectural Diagrams & Site Analysis
