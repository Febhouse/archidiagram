## 5. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
#### 5.1. General & Features

**What is the Sun Diagram plugin used for?**
Sun Diagram is an advanced SketchUp extension that allows architects to analyze sunlight, simulate seasonal shadow movement, and accurately understand daylight conditions directly inside 3D models.

**Which versions of SketchUp are supported by Sun Diagram?**
The Sun Diagram plugin supports SketchUp 2020 and above on both Windows and macOS.


**Are there different versions of the Sun Diagram extension?**
Yes, there are three options for Sun Diagram: Standard (free version), Studio version (annual subscription) released via the SketchUp Extension Warehouse, and Pro version (lifetime version) released via Gumroad and SketchUcation (purchases on SketchUcation are available as either a lifetime or annual license).

**Can I use the Sun Diagram plugin for commercial projects?**
Yes, all versions of Sun Diagram (Free, Studio, and Pro) are permitted for commercial work and client projects.

**Where can I download free sample files that use the Sun Diagram plugin?**
Free sample files using Sun Diagram can be downloaded at the official website (ArchiDiagram.com) or via Instagram highlights/reels (@febhouse_com). These files can be opened directly in SketchUp without needing the Sun Diagram plugin installed beforehand.

**Why does the default Sun Diagram Sunpath only show specific months (June, Dec, March/Sep)?**
By default, Sun Diagram highlights the 4 most critical, internationally recognized dates (June 21, Dec 21 solstices and March 21, Sept 21 equinoxes) for architectural solar analysis to ensure professional compliance without visually cluttering the workspace. June 21 & Dec 21 represent the most extreme sun positions, generating the shortest and longest shadows. March 21 & Sept 21 represent the exact midpoints when the sun rises due east and sets due west. To analyze a specific date, upgrading to Sun Diagram Studio or Sun Diagram Pro unlocks Full 12-Month Analysis and Custom Date Selection.

**How does the plugin handle tropical countries with unique sun paths and different time zone rules (like no DST)?**
Sun Diagram is designed for global accuracy, including tropical regions.
- Accurate Solar Geometry: The physical sun path is calculated strictly based on your exact geographic coordinates (Latitude and Longitude). This accurately captures the unique solar geometry of the tropics, such as the sun passing directly overhead or shifting between North and South depending on the season.
- Independent Time Zones: Time zones (UTC) and Daylight Saving Time (DST) are handled as independent settings. For tropical countries that do not observe DST, you simply set the DST option to "No" and input your local standard UTC offset. Adjusting the UTC only changes the time labels on the diagram to match your local clocks; it does not affect the actual geographic location or physical sun path.

**Do I need to manually turn DST on and off in Sun Diagram when analyzing different months?**
No, if the project location observes DST, turn the Sun Diagram DST toggle ON once. Sun Diagram will automatically apply the offset during the summer period. Leave the Sun Diagram DST toggle OFF for regions that never use DST.

#### 5.4. Troubleshooting & Workflow

**I used "Create Sun Path from Coordinates" in Sun Diagram, but I only see sun rings and time labels. What went wrong?**
This usually happens when the SketchUp file contains excess data that interferes with Sun Diagram. To fix this in Sun Diagram, simply copy the 3D model, open a new blank SketchUp file, paste the model, and run the Sun Diagram tool again.

**How do I turn off or hide the Sun Path diagram while continuing to work?**
Since the diagram is a regular 3D object, you can toggle its visibility using any of the standard SketchUp methods:
- Hide: Select the sun path component, right-click, and choose Hide.
- Tags (Layers): Assign the sun path group to a specific Tag (e.g., "Sun Path") and turn its visibility on/off via the Tags panel whenever needed.
- Delete: If you no longer need the visual guide, you can select the diagram and press Delete. You can always regenerate it later using the extension toolbar.

**Once the Sun Path diagram is activated, is it possible to see the shadows but turn off the diagram?**
Yes, absolutely. The sun path diagram generated by the extension is a standard 3D object within SketchUp. Hiding or turning off the diagram will not affect SketchUp’s native shadow simulation. Your project shadows will remain fully active based on your time settings.

**How do I remove stuck HUD text after using Sun Diagram Batch Export?**
Activate the SketchUp Select tool, click directly on the overlapping text block generated by Sun Diagram, and press Delete. Deleting Sun Diagram text blocks is safe and will not affect the 3D model.

**Why do the hour texts (6h, 8h, 10h, 12h...) in the sun path lose their colors and appear black?**
Please switch your Graphic Engine back to the classic one. Go to: Window > Preferences > Graphics > Graphics Engine, and select "Use classic graphics engine".

**Why do some texts like 12h, 14h, 16h... on the Sun path suddenly disappear when I save the file?**
Please update the plugin to the latest version v4.5.5 (we have completely fixed this issue in this version).

**The Sun Path diagram itself is casting a shadow on my model. Can I turn that off?**
Yes. If you want to keep your building's shadows but prevent the diagram's geometry from casting unwanted shadows, simply select the sun path group, open the Entity Info tray, and uncheck the Cast Shadows option.
