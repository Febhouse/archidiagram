Creating compelling architectural section perspectives is one of the most effective ways to communicate spatial relationships and interior lighting. However, managing section cuts while maintaining accurate, visually appealing shadows inside your SketchUp model has traditionally been a tedious workaround.

That’s exactly why [**Shadow Slice 2.0**](/shadow-slice/) was developed, to completely automate and enhance this workflow.

## What Makes Shadow Slice 2.0 Different?

As you can see in the video, Shadow Slice 2.0 doesn't just cut your model; it intelligently manages how light and geometry interact at the section plane. If you are preparing architectural diagrams or presentation documents, here is how the plugin streamlines your process:

- **Dynamic Shadow Generation:** Shadows instantly calculate and cast correctly within the sectioned interior spaces, adding immediate depth to your perspectives.
- **Total Stylistic Control:** Using the Shadow Slice Manager, you can instantly toggle section planes, adjust section line widths, and customize the section fill color (like the distinct red fill highlighted in the video) to match your specific ArchiDiagram templates and brand aesthetics.
- **Live Updates:** As you orbit or adjust the sun settings, the section cuts and shadows respond dynamically, saving you hours of manual scene updating and post-production in Canva or Photoshop.
