# Refund Policy — Febhouse

At Febhouse, your satisfaction is our top priority. We want you to feel completely confident when purchasing our products.

### **I. SketchUp Plugins (Digital Extensions)**

Since our plugins are available through different platforms, the refund process depends on where you made your purchase:

- **1. Purchases via Gumroad (e.g., Pro Plugins):**
    
    
    - **License &amp; Delivery:** Delivered instantly via Gumroad with a lifetime license. Activation requires only a one-time internet connection to enter your license key. After that, the plugin works fully offline.
    - **7-Day Money-Back Guarantee:** We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee. If the plugin doesn't work on your setup or you're not happy with it, let us know within 7 days.
    - **How Refunds Work:** We’ll work with you to troubleshoot any issues first. If we can’t get it working, we’ll issue a refund (minus small non-refundable payment processing fees ~10%).
- **2. Purchases via SketchUp Extension Warehouse (e.g., Studio Plugins):**
    
    
    - **License &amp; Delivery:** Processed and managed directly by Trimble. Licensing is securely tied to your Trimble ID.
    - **Refund Policy:** Purchases made directly through the Extension Warehouse are subject to Trimble’s official billing and refund policies. Refund requests or subscription cancellations must be handled through your Trimble Account portal.

*💡 **Note:** We strongly recommend trying the **Free version** of our plugins first — if it runs smoothly, the paid versions will work the same way in almost all cases.*

### **II. Canva Templates &amp; Other Instant Digital Downloads**

- Due to the instant delivery nature of Canva templates, graphics packs, and similar digital files (you receive editable links or downloadable files immediately after purchase), **we do not offer refunds or exchanges** on these products once the purchase is completed.
- We encourage you to carefully review the product previews, descriptions, and included files before purchasing.

### **III. How To Get Help Or Request A Refund**

- **For Gumroad Purchases:** Simply reply to your Gumroad purchase receipt email, or contact us directly at **Info@Febhouse.com**.
- **For Extension Warehouse Purchases:** For billing or refund inquiries, please use the Trimble Account management portal. For technical support with the plugin itself, contact us at **Info@Febhouse.com**.

We’ll get back to you quickly to help fix the problem.

**Thank you for choosing Febhouse! 🚀**
