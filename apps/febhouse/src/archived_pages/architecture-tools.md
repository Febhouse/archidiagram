## Architecture tools

Explore **Architecture tools** — Sun Diagram and Dynamic Symbols — **designed for architects**. Available free on Extension Warehouse.

 <figure>[![sun diagram](/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/febhouse.png)](https://sundiagram.com/)</figure>### [Sun Diagram](https://sundiagram.com/)

Sun Diagram is a **SketchUp extension (plugin)** designed to create 3D Sun paths and analyze sunlight for architectural projects.  
**Sun Diagram has 4 Features** **1. Create Sunpath from Coordinates**, with support for Daylight Saving Time adjustments.  
**2. Create Shadow Analysis**: Simulate shadows and sun positions instantly for any selected time frame — no guessing or manual setup.  
**3. Export Shadow Analysis**: Batch export shadow and Sun position images with a single click.   
**4. Style Library :** Find the perfect style for your project — instantly, with just one click. .  
*Sun Diagram is an upgraded version of 3D Sunpath Chart, designed to enhance your workflow even further.*

 <figure>[![dynamic symbols](/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/dynamicsymbols.png)](https://dynamicsymbols.com/)</figure>### [Dynamic Symbols](https://dynamicsymbols.com/)

Dynamic Symbols is a **SketchUp extension (plugin)** that allows you to create animated symbols and switch between different states with just one click.  
**Dynamic Symbols has 3 Features:** **1. One-Click Symbol Change — Keep Position, Size &amp; Rotation**   
Dynamic Symbols lets you instantly switch between different symbol shapes with just one click — while keeping the symbol’s position, size, and rotation unchanged..   
**2.** **One-Click Color Change – Keep Position &amp; Orientation**   
Change the color of your symbols instantly with one click — no need to open complex menus or edit materials manually.   
**3. Create Animation** Automatically generate animated scenes that bring your symbols to life — ideal for diagrams, flowcharts, or architectural storytelling.
