# About Febhouse

 ![Architecture Meets Technology](/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/cover.jpg)## Introduction

**Febhouse** is an independent architectural studio that develops design projects and professional digital tools to support architects in visualization, analysis, and communication.

We focus on connecting architecture and technology through both built work and software development, with a strong emphasis on diagram clarity, sunlight analysis, and sustainable design thinking.

 <figure>[![NamNH](/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NAMNH-1920-1024x572.jpg)](https://gravatar.com/nghanam)</figure>### [Founder](https://gravatar.com/nghanam)

**Mr. Nam Nguyen (Nguyễn Hà Nam) — Architect &amp; Software Developer.** Nam is an architect who develops professional SketchUp extensions and diagram systems to improve how architects study sunlight, environmental conditions, and conceptual design.   
  
He is the creator of **<u> [ Sun Diagram ](https://sundiagram.com/)</u>**, **<u> [ Dynamic Symbols ](https://Dynamicsymbols.com/)</u>**, and **<u> [ Shadow Slice ](https://archidiagram.com/shadow-slice/)</u>**, — tools used by architects worldwide for visualization and architectural workflows.   
  
He also shares tutorials, templates, and resources through ArchiDiagram.com, helping architects improve their diagram skills and presentation methods.

###  

## Philosophy

We believe architects should spend their time **designing, not battling software**. Born from real-world architectural practice, our philosophy is to bridge the gap between complex spatial ideas and clear visual communication. We build intuitive, smart tools that remove technical barriers, allowing designers to focus entirely on what matters most: creativity and spatial storytelling.

## Recognition

**Febhouse** is a **Verified SketchUp Extension Vendor** on the ![SketchUp Extensions Logo](/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/download.png) [**SketchUp Extension Warehouse**](https://extensions.sketchup.com/developer/582b09fa-9fe2-4248-b9e8-80dc7af69a76).

Our extensions, including [**Sun Diagram**](https://sundiagram.com/), [**Dynamic Symbols**](https://dynamicsymbols.com/), and [**Shadow Slice**](https://archidiagram.com/shadow-slice/), are used by architects, urban designers, and students worldwide for sunlight analysis, visualization, and architectural diagram production.
