# Architecture Tools and Design Studio

Febhouse is an **architecture and technology studio** that designs architectural projects and develops **professional SketchUp extensions** used by architects worldwide.

We help architects **visualize, analyze sunlight, and communicate design ideas more clearly.**

 [ Explore Our Work ](/architecture-projects/)## What We Do - Design and Technology, Combined

### Architecture &amp; Landscape Design

 https://www.youtube.com/shorts/yILCdMfaE1IWe **design** architectural and landscape projects that respond to climate, context, and environmental conditions — combining functionality, sustainability, and clear design thinking.

 [ View Projects ](/architecture-projects/)### Architecture Tools

 https://www.youtube.com/shorts/LILJFNDNSKU [ Get Architecture Tools ](/architecture-tools/)We develop **professional SketchUp extensions** that help architects **visualize sunlight, automate diagrams, and improve workflows.**

Our tools — including **[Sun Diagram](https://sundiagram.com/), [Dynamic Symbols](https://dynamicsymbols.com/), and [Shadow Slice](https://archidiagram.com/shadow-slice/)** — are published on the [SketchUp Extension Warehouse](https://extensions.sketchup.com/developer/582b09fa-9fe2-4248-b9e8-80dc7af69a76) and used by **architects, designers, and students worldwide.**

 [ Visit ArchiDiagram ](https://archidiagram.com/)## About Febhouse

Febhouse is an independent Architecture &amp; Technology Studio based in Hanoi.

We work at the intersection of design and innovation, combining architectural expertise with software development to create tools, visual systems, and workflows that enhance how architects design and present.

[**Official SketchUp Extension Vendor on SketchUp Extension Warehouse**](https://extensions.sketchup.com/developer/582b09fa-9fe2-4248-b9e8-80dc7af69a76)

Our mission is to make architectural design smarter, faster, and more sustainable.
