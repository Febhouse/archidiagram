## THE LEAF TOWN ECOLOGICAL PARK (BAO LOC - DA LAT)

The Leaf Town Ecological Park is developed in collaboration with investor Phuong Dong. This is an extensive park situated on a hillside with an area of 1 hectare, serving the residents within the project.

The natural landscape here is stunning and majestic, so the park focuses on utility blocks that maximize scenic viewpoints and leverage the local terrain.

### LANDSCAPE CROSS-SECTION

First, let's look at the longitudinal landscape cross-section to see the complexity of the local terrain. The elevation varies from -12,000 to +12,000. The 0.000 mark is placed at the center of the project, which is also the entrance location for the convenience of guests.

 ![Mặt cắt cảnh quan của công viên sinh thái](/images/2023/05/MC-1-1_2-1920x900-1-scaled.jpg.webp)

### OVERALL PERSPECTIVE

Experience the diversity of utility spaces within the park through a bird's-eye view.

### PROJECT HIGHLIGHTS

This area will host music events with outdoor seating stretching from the stage area up to the camping grounds (elevation +12,000). Beneath the camping ground is the restroom area and water supply system for the entire project.

 ![Phối cảnh từ trên cao khu săn mây của công viên sinh thái](/images/2023/05/20230516-CV-1_11-1024x576.jpg) ![Phối cảnh khu camping và sân khấu trong khu công viên sinh thái](/images/2023/05/20230516-CV-1_8-1024x576.jpg)

Next is the central area featuring the project's nameplate and the clubhouse—boasting open architecture and a roof shape that is a signature of this development. Adjacent to the Clubhouse is a children's playground and a pet garden, creating a great attraction for little ones.

 ![Phối cảnh nhà clubhouse](/images/2023/05/20230516-CV-1_4-1024x576.jpg) ![Phối cảnh tổng thể nhà clubhouse của công viên sinh thái](/images/2023/05/20230516-CV-1_1.jpg-1024x576.webp)
