## NEO CLASSICAL ECOLOGICAL HOUSE

The Neo Classical Ecological House is a harmonious blend of neo-classical architectural elegance and high-tech ecological architecture standards.

 ![ảnh động mặt cắt qua các tầng nhà tân cổ sinh thái](/images/2023/08/CHUY-1920.gif)

### Nhà tân cổ điển sinh thái

### A Prime Location

The house boasts two stunning frontages. The main West-facing facade borders National Route 1A, offering convenient transportation and seamless connection to the city. Meanwhile, the East-facing facade overlooks a beautiful lake, creating a relaxing, peaceful, and comfortable living space. This is the ideal setting to build a perfect home.

### Ecological Architecture Design – Living in Harmony with Nature

This project applies high-tech ecological design principles. We utilize advanced thermal insulation materials, such as Low-E glass and heat-resistant paint, combined with thick wall structures. These features significantly reduce the impact of solar radiation in a tropical climate.

### Solar Energy Integration

To optimize energy consumption and minimize electricity costs, the project incorporates rooftop solar panels. Harnessing solar energy not only protects the environment but also provides a clean and sustainable power source for the home.

### Maximizing the Scenic Lake View

To fully capture the beautiful lake view, our architects proposed an atrium (double-height space) with large glass panels in the living room. This allows the family and their guests to enjoy the lake view from multiple angles, creating memorable moments of relaxation.

### Premium Amenities for Living Spaces

The homeowner desired a top-tier, fully equipped living space. In response, our architects designed two master suites with stunning lake views. These master bedrooms are equipped with modern amenities, including a private gym, a sauna, and a relaxing garden, offering a luxurious lifestyle and a perfect retreat after a stressful workday.
