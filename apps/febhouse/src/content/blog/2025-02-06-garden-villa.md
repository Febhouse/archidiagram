## ECO-GARDEN VILLA

The Eco-Garden Villa is a harmonious blend of neo-classical architectural style and high-tech ecological architecture standards.

 ![Nhà vườn](/images/2025/02/20241214-OP2-V20-resized.jpg.webp)

## A Prime Location

The house boasts two stunning frontages. The main West-facing facade borders National Route 1A, offering convenient transportation and seamless connection to the city. Meanwhile, the East-facing facade overlooks a beautiful lake, creating a relaxing, peaceful, and comfortable living space. This is the ideal setting to build a perfect home.

### Ecological Architecture Design – Living in Harmony with Nature

This project applies high-tech ecological design principles. We utilize advanced thermal insulation materials, such as Low-E glass, heat-resistant paint, and thick wall structures. These features significantly reduce the impact of solar radiation in a tropical climate.

### Solar Energy Integration

To optimize energy consumption and minimize electricity costs, the project incorporates rooftop solar panels. Harnessing solar energy not only protects the environment but also provides a clean and sustainable power source for the villa.

### Maximizing the Scenic Lake View

To fully capture the beautiful lake view and maximize spatial quality, our architects proposed an atrium (double-height space) with large glass panels in the living room. This allows the homeowners and their guests to enjoy the lake view from multiple angles, creating memorable moments of relaxation.

### Premium Amenities for Living Spaces

The homeowner desired a top-tier, fully equipped living space. In response, our architects designed two master suites with stunning lake views. These master bedrooms are equipped with modern amenities, including a private gym, a sauna, and a relaxing garden, offering a luxurious lifestyle and a perfect retreat after a stressful workday.
