## KIM BOI RETREAT HOMESTAY

This weekend retreat was designed for a Korean client on a 2,600 m2 site in Kim Boi, Hoa Binh. The project consists of minimalist one-story houses using affordable, rustic materials. However, this simplicity challenged us to dive deeper into the purity of architectural form—creating clean, neat shapes with golden ratios to bring out the intrinsic beauty of the structures.

### MODEL A - SEMI-DETACHED VILLA

Each unit features 2 bedrooms, 1 living room, and a dining area. The space between the two houses serves as a shared outdoor courtyard for common activities.

## THE CLUBHOUSE

The central hub functions as an entertainment zone (karaoke), a dining kitchen, a locker area, a sauna, and a covered outdoor dining space.
