## D-HOUSE: AN ANCESTRAL HOUSE RETREAT

 ![Nhà thờ họ](/images/2025/02/PHOICANH_TONGTHE_1-resized-1024x576.jpg)

## Design Inspiration

While "Nhà thờ họ" (Ancestral House) is the formal name of this project, its purpose extends far beyond tradition. This is a gathering place for the entire family—a peaceful retreat to find tranquility during holidays and weekends.

### Site Planning &amp; Landscape

The master plan meticulously balances traditional spiritual space with modern living comfort.

- **Main Ancestral Hall:** The heart of the project, designed with traditional architectural proportions.
- **Living Annex:** Includes 2 bedrooms, a kitchen, and a dining area, ensuring the family has a comfortable space for extended stays.
- **Ecological Garden:** Features a lotus pond, ancient star fruit trees, and various fruit trees (Areca, Grapefruit), creating a microclimate that cools the entire site naturally.
- **Outdoor Amenities:** Includes a dedicated area for outdoor cooking (traditional wood-fire cooking), a relaxation yard, and a multi-functional courtyard.

 ![mặt bằng mái nhà thờ họ](/images/2025/02/20240410_NHATHO_Page_03-copy-1024x724.jpg) ![mặt bằng tổng thể nhà thờ họ](/images/2025/02/20240410_NHATHO_Page_04-copy-1024x724.jpg)

## Architectural Philosophy

D-House is an example of how traditional values can be preserved within a modern ecological framework. By utilizing natural ventilation and a rich green landscape, the project minimizes the need for artificial cooling while maintaining a serene, rustic atmosphere.
