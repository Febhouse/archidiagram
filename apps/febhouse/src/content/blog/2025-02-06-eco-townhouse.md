## ECO-TOWNHOUSE ARCHITECTURE

The Eco-Townhouse is a harmonious blend of contemporary architectural style and high-tech ecological building standards.

https://youtu.be/WM53lYvbZ1g ### A Prime Location

The house boasts an impressive 9m wide frontage. Located within an urban residential area with a 7.5m wide road in front, this site provides the ideal foundation for a beautiful and perfect home.

### Eco-Home Design – Living in Harmony with Nature

This project applies high-tech ecological design principles. We utilize advanced heat-resistant materials, such as Low-E glass and thermal insulation coatings, combined with thick wall layers. These features significantly reduce the impact of solar radiation in a tropical climate.

### Solar Energy Integration

To optimize energy consumption and minimize electricity costs, the project incorporates rooftop solar panels. This utilization of solar energy not only protects the environment but also creates a clean and sustainable power source for the home.

### Maximizing the 9m Frontage

To make the most of the 9m frontage, our architects proposed extending the 2nd-floor master bedroom across the entire width of the house. To ensure privacy and security, a large louvre system was designed to cover the facade when needed. The first floor is also protected by a discreet fence that integrates seamlessly with glass elements.

### Premium Amenities for Living Spaces

While every room is designed to receive natural light, the master bedroom already occupies the main frontage. Therefore, we introduced a rear skylight to bring natural light and ventilation (air circulation) into the heart of the home, reconnecting the interior with nature.
