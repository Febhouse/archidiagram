## A Charming Little Spot in the Old Quarter

 ![quán kem nhỏ xinh](/images/2025/02/20240131_EXTERIOR_1-1920-1024x576.jpg)

The design inspiration for this project stems from the shop's signature dish: Pandan Sticky Rice (Kem xôi nếp). Our architects utilized the distinct color and fragrance of pandan leaves to create a unique and recognizable brand identity for the space.

### Key Color Palette:

- **Dominant Color:** Pandan Green (covering 60% of the interior area).
- **Accent Colors:** Rustic wood tones (used for window frames, furniture, and wall accents) to create a warm, natural yet sophisticated atmosphere.

 ![định hướng quán kem nhỏ xinh](/images/2025/02/20240315_MATSE2-OP3_Page_03-copy-1024x726.jpg)

## Interior &amp; Exterior Philosophy:

- - **Interior:** We used soft, textured green tones combined with circular design elements (inspired by scoops of ice cream) to create a friendly and inviting mood.
    - **Exterior:** The facade features a prominent "Pandan Green" wall with arched windows and a natural wood-grain ceiling system to conceal the old structure and modernize the street presence.
    - **Space Optimization:** Despite being a small lot in a busy city area, the layout maximizes seating by utilizing a "street-side" bench concept, allowing customers to enjoy the vibrant Hanoi atmosphere.
