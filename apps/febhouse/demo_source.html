Help Center & Technical FAQ

Find quick answers to common questions about our extensions, tutorials, and architectural resources.

Need instant answers?
What is ArchiDiagram.com?

ArchiDiagram.com is an online platform that provides architectural diagram resources, free tutorials, templates, symbols, and workflow tools to help architects present design ideas clearly and more efficiently.

Are all resources on ArchiDiagram.com free?

Many resourcesâincluding tutorials, sample files, and basic diagram templatesâare completely free. Some advanced tools and premium templates may require purchase through Gumroad.

Do I need SketchUp to use the diagram templates?

Not all templates require SketchUp. ArchiDiagram provides files for various formats, including SketchUp (.skp), Photoshop (.psd), and Canva templates. You can choose the format that fits your workflow.

What are Sun Diagram and Dynamic Symbols?

They are professional architectural plugins (offering Free, Studio, and Pro versions). Sun Diagram provides sun path + shadow analysis for SketchUp. Dynamic Symbols provides smart diagram symbols for faster architectural presentations. Both plugins support the diagram workflows featured on ArchiDiagram.com.

Do I need to use the Pro or Studio versions of the plugins to follow the tutorials?

No. Most tutorials can be completed without the paid versions. However, using Sun Diagram (Pro/Studio) and Dynamic Symbols (Pro/Studio) will produce more accurate diagrams and reduce the amount of manual work required.

Can I use the resources for commercial projects?

Yes. All free templates and symbols can be used for commercial architectural projects. However, the sample files provided on the website are based on real projects, so you may not include them in your portfolio or professional profile unless you have received explicit permission from us.

Where can I find examples of architectural diagrams?

You can explore real examples through Tutorial pages, Free sample files, and Instagram / YouTube tutorials published by Febhouse. These samples show complete workflows from concept to final diagram.

How do I download the free templates / free sample files?

For resources hosted on Gumroad with a '$0+' price, you can download them for free by entering 0 in the price box and clicking 'I want this!'. For resources distributed via Instagram, simply comment on the corresponding post, and we will send you a Google Drive download link through DM.

Who is behind ArchiDiagram.com?

ArchiDiagram is developed and maintained by Nam Nguyen (Nguyen Ha Nam) â the founder of Febhouse. He is an Architect and Software Developer specializing in SketchUp extensions and architectural visualization tools.

For general support, custom diagram inquiries, or file issues, email info@febhouse.com. Support is available daily.

How does Sun Diagram calculate solar positions?

Sun Diagram uses astronomical solar equations based on latitude and longitude coordinates to calculate real-world solar azimuth and altitude angles for any hour, day, and month of the year.
