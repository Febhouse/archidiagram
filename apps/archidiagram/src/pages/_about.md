# About ArchiDiagram

**ArchiDiagram.com** is an online platform that provides architecture students, designers, and professionals with practical resources for creating clear, impactful architectural diagrams.

Founded by [**Nam Nguyen (Nguyễn Hà Nam)**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/namnguyen-febhouse/), an architect, creator of [**Febhouse Studio**](https://febhouse.com/), and a [**Verified SketchUp Extension Vendor**](https://extensions.sketchup.com/developer/582b09fa-9fe2-4248-b9e8-80dc7af69a76), ArchiDiagram was born from a simple idea:

📌 *“Architectural diagrams should be easy, intuitive, and accessible for everyone—whether you're working on a school project or professional presentation.”*

![Nam Nguyen](/images/2026/08/NAMNH-1920-1024x572.jpg)

## Our Mission

At ArchiDiagram, our mission is to help architects communicate their ideas more clearly.  
We do this by offering:

- Free tutorials
- Presentation templates
- Diagram symbols &amp; assets
- Workflow tools
- SketchUp plugins that speed up diagram creation

All resources are designed to make the diagram-making process **faster, cleaner, and more professional**.

## Our Tools &amp; Ecosystem

 ![Febhouse-ecosystem](/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/FEBHOUSE-ECOSYSTEM-1536x847.jpg)ArchiDiagram is part of the Febhouse ecosystem—developed by a [**Verified SketchUp Extension Vendor**](https://extensions.sketchup.com/developer/582b09fa-9fe2-4248-b9e8-80dc7af69a76)—which includes:

- [**Sun Diagram**](https://sundiagram.com/) a SketchUp plugin for visualizing sun paths, solar exposure, and shadow simulations.
- [**Dynamic Symbols**](https://dynamicsymbols.com/) a lightweight plugin that helps create editable, animated symbols for architectural diagrams.
- [**Shadow Slice:**](/shadow-slice/) Interior Shadow Analysis &amp; Sun Study Tool for SketchUp
- [**Architectural Templates Presentation**](/#Presentations) Ready-to-use templates to simplify your presentation workflow.

Together, these tools help architects present ideas with clarity and confidence.

## The Story Behind ArchiDiagram

As an architect and content creator, Nam spent years producing architectural diagrams for real projects, competitions, and tutorials on platforms like YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok.

Throughout this journey, he noticed one problem:

*👉 Most architects struggled with diagram presentation, even when their ideas were strong.*

This led to the creation of ArchiDiagram in 2021 — a place where anyone can learn, download templates, and improve their design communication skills in a practical way.

## Who Uses ArchiDiagram?

ArchiDiagram has become a trusted resource for:

- Architecture students
- Design studios
- Educators
- Urban planners
- 3D visualization artists
- Anyone learning to communicate ideas visually
