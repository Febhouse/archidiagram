## Why Analyze High-Density Sites?

In the city, your site is defined by its neighbors. Use this diagram style to clearly show:

- **Solar Access:** Exactly how neighboring towers block sunlight throughout the year.
- **Wind & Circulation:** How air flows between buildings using clean, directional arrows.
- **Site Prominence:** Make your specific plot (in red) pop against the gray urban mass.
