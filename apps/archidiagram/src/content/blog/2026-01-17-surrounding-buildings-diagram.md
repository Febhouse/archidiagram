## Why Analyze Surrounding Buildings?

Context analysis is more than just a 2D map—it's about understanding 3D relationships. By modeling the surrounding massing, you can clearly communicate:

- **Scale & Proportion:** How your design fits within the existing skyline.
- **Privacy & Enclosure:** Identifying which sides of the site are overlooked by tall neighbors.
- **Solar Access:** Exactly how adjacent buildings cast shadows onto your site throughout the year.

## What This Sample Focuses On

This sample demonstrates a minimal yet highly effective workflow to analyze:

### **1. Heights and Massing**

Visualizing the volume and density of the urban fabric. The diagram uses simplified "sugar cube" geometry to keep the focus strictly on scale rather than distracting architectural details.

### **2. Solar Obstruction & Sun Domes**

Unlike simple shadow casting, this diagram visualizes the **Solar Dome** to show the sun's trajectory relative to building heights. 
*(**Pro Tip:** The translucent sun dome and solar path shown in this sample are generated using the **Sun Diagram** plugin. It’s the fastest way to visualize complex solar data in 3D).*

### **3. Contextual Zoning**

Using color-coded layers to distinguish between the site, immediate context, and distant landmarks. 
*(**Workflow Note:** To annotate these zones clearly with consistent text and leaders, we recommend using **Dynamic Symbols**).*
