# Small 1-Story Townhouse Design: Free Daylight Optimization Sample (Sample - 63)

Great architecture isn't just for luxury villas with massive budgets. Smart design is even more critical in compact, budget-friendly projects. 

This sample (Project 63) provides the source files for a **Small 1-Story Townhouse** (a typical "tube house"), proving how careful solar analysis can drastically improve the living experience without adding a dime to construction costs.

When space is limited, optimizing **natural light** and **passive design** is the secret to making a small home feel spacious, healthy, and comfortable.

 ![Daylight Optimization](/images/2026/03/DECEMBER21st-9am.webp)

## Why Analyze Small & Budget-Friendly Homes?

Townhouses typically share walls with neighbors, meaning natural light can only enter from the front and back. Without careful planning, the core of the house becomes dark and damp. By running a daylight study early on, you can:

- **Enhance Spatial Perception:** Natural light makes tight spaces feel instantly larger.
- **Reduce Energy Bills:** Strategically placing clerestory windows or skylights minimizes the need for daytime artificial lighting and AC.
- **Improve Well-being:** Tracking how the sun enters the living areas ensures a healthier indoor environment year-round.

## What This Sample Focuses On

This SketchUp file gives you a clear workflow for testing daylight strategies in narrow plots:

### **1. Deep Light Penetration**

The model features a slanted roof with clerestory windows designed to pull light straight into the center of the deep floor plan. Our analysis proves whether this window sizing actually works.

### **2. The "Sliced" Interior View**

To see how light interacts with the space, we have to look inside. 
*(**Workflow Secret:** Instead of hiding the roof—which would ruin the shadow calculations—we used the **Shadow Slice** plugin. It cleanly cuts the upper geometry, allowing you to see true interior shadow patterns on the floor plan).*

### **3. Winter Solstice Animation**

The setup specifically tests December 21st (the winter solstice, when the sun is at its lowest). 
*(**Pro Tip:** The animated 3D sun path and solar tracking are driven by the **Sun Diagram** plugin, making it super easy to export smooth sun-study videos).*
