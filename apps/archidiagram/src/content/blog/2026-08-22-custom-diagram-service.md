## How We Work

Our process is simple and focused:

1. We review your site and project scope
2. We set up accurate site orientation and real sun data
3. We produce clean, presentation-ready diagrams
4. We deliver final outputs in **PDF Report** format, along with source files (**SketchUp**)

The diagrams follow the **same structure and visual language** as the sample files available on ArchiDiagram.

## What We Do NOT Provide

To avoid misunderstanding, please note:

- We do **not** create conceptual massing or architectural form diagrams
- We do **not** illustrate design ideas or building concepts

Our service is strictly limited to:

- **Site analysis**
- **Sun and shadow diagrams**
- **Impact verification**

## What You Need to Provide

To ensure accurate analysis and reliable results, we require one of the following as input:

- **Floor plans of all relevant levels**
- **Elevations** (with dimensions or clear scale)
- **A 3D model** (SketchUp or compatible formats), If you already have a 3D model, it can significantly reduce setup time. If not, we can recreate the geometry based on your drawings.
- Please note: We require accurate site data to ensure reliability. We **cannot** process projects provided only via Google Maps links without **architectural drawings**, as our work is strictly based on **factual data, not estimation**
