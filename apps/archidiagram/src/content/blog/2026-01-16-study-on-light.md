## Why Perform a Light Study?

A static shadow setting isn't enough to inform good design. A true light study helps you:

- **Validate Orientation:** Ensure your building captures natural light exactly where it’s needed.
- **Analyze Seasonality:** Compare the harsh summer sun against the low winter sun.
- **Contextual Impact:** See exactly how surrounding buildings cast shadows onto your site.
