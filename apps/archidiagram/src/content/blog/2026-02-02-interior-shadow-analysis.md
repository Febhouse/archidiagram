# Interior Shadow Analysis in SketchUp: Free Natural Light Study Sample (Project 62) 

A beautiful façade is great, but the actual quality of life inside a building depends entirely on **Natural Light**. 

This sample project (Project 62) demonstrates a specialized **Interior Shadow Analysis** workflow. It allows you to visualize exactly how sunlight penetrates living spaces throughout the day, ensuring you get the most out of your window placement.

Unlike standard shadow studies that only look at the exterior, this SketchUp model uses a clever "sliced" view technique to reveal the floor plan below.

 ![Interior Shadow Analysis](/images/2026/02/Dec11-1920.jpg)

## Why Analyze Interior Natural Light?

Understanding how light moves inside a room is critical for:

- **Visual Comfort:** Spotting potential glare on TV screens or workspaces.
- **Passive Heating:** Seeing how deep the winter sun reaches to warm the floors.
- **Functionality:** Ensuring kitchen counters and reading nooks get the daylight they need.

## What This Sample Focuses On

### **1. The "Sliced" Roof Technique**

To analyze the interior without hiding the shadows from the roof overhangs, we need to look *through* the roof. 
*(**Workflow Secret:** We used the **Shadow Slice** plugin for this. It lets you cut through the upper geometry (like roofs or ceilings) to create a clear plan view, while still casting correct shadows on the floor).*

### **2. Accurate Solar Trajectory**

The house is surrounded by a precise **3D Sun Path**, pinpointing the sun's exact position for specific dates (like the June 21st solstice).

### **3. Motion & Animation Ready**

Static images are nice, but moving shadows tell the real story. This setup is perfectly optimized for exporting solar animations. 
*(**Tool Used:** We used **Sun Diagram** to set up the animation keyframes, letting you export smooth "sun study" videos directly from this view).*

## Workflow Overview

You can replicate this "Interior Daylight" style by following these steps:

1. **Model the Context:** Set up the house and neighboring buildings.
2. **Generate Sun Path:** Use **Sun Diagram** to visualize the solar angles.
3. **Slice the View:** Use **Shadow Slice** to cut the top of the building, revealing the furniture and partition layout below.
4. **Animate:** Use **Sun Diagram** to play the timeline and watch how light sweeps across the room.

## Essential Tools for This Workflow

To achieve this specific look—seeing inside the house while accurately tracking the sun—we recommend:

- **Shadow Slice:** The absolute best tool for cutting sections and looking through obstacles without permanently deleting geometry.
- **Sun Diagram:** Essential for generating the 3D sun path and smooth solar animations.
