Traditional architectural diagrams are great for clarity, but sometimes your presentation needs to make a bold, futuristic statement. 

If you want to create a stunning **AI Architecture diagram**, this sample (AI Sample 01) introduces a powerful workflow that bridges standard 3D modeling with Generative AI.

This sample provides the exact AI Prompt needed to transform a standard, daytime SketchUp export into a striking, dark-mode analytical diagram featuring glowing neon lines and a highly stylized urban context.

## Why Use AI for Architectural Diagrams?

This prompt is carefully engineered to recognize standard architectural annotations and convert them into glowing elements. It focuses on two main transformations:

### 1. Dark Mode Urban Fabric

The prompt suppresses the daytime textures of your base image, converting the surrounding city context into a deep, moody, and sleek background to create depth.

### 2. Neon Environmental Forces

Standard arrows and lines are translated into glowing tubes of light:
- **Cyan Neon:** Emphasizes airflow, wind corridors, and movement.
- **Yellow Neon:** Highlights the sun path, solar trajectory, and key time markers.
- **Red Neon:** Defines property boundaries and critical site constraints.

## The SketchUp to AI Workflow

To get the best results when generating an **AI Architecture diagram**, your base image needs clear, well-structured data.

1. **Prepare the Base:** Export a clean analytical diagram from SketchUp. 
*(**Pro Tip:** Use the [**Sun Diagram**](https://sundiagram.com/) plugin for the solar dome and [**Dynamic Symbols**](https://dynamicsymbols.com/) for the wind arrows to ensure the AI has clear, distinct shapes to "trace" and illuminate).*
2. **Input to AI:** Upload your base image to your preferred AI generator.
3. **Apply the Prompt:** Paste the provided prompt below, blending your image with the text instructions.
4. **Refine:** Watch the AI transform your standard export into a luminous presentation piece!

## The Core AI Prompt

*(Prompt designed for Gemini - Nano Banana)*

> **ROLE & TASK:**  
> Act as a professional architectural illustrator. Your task is to re-style the attached diagram image into a high-quality 3D visualization while strictly maintaining its informational structure.  
> 
> **1. INPUT ANALYSIS:**  
> Analyze the provided image. Identify the layout, the connections (arrows/lines), the text labels, and the hierarchical organization of the boxes.  
> 
> **2. STRICT CONSTRAINTS (DO NOT CHANGE):**  
> Geometry: You must preserve the EXACT layout and position of all elements. Do not move boxes or rearrange arrows. Do not add new elements (near houses, contour lines..).  
> Text: Keep all original text labels visible and legible. Do not change the content of the text.  
> Clarity: The connections (paths/arrows) must be distinct and follow the original.  
> Circle Logo in note: keep original.  
> Keep the aspect ratio.  
> 
> **3. STYLE PARAMETERS (APPLY THIS STYLE):**  
> Atmosphere: Futuristic, clean, X-ray diagram aesthetic, dark mode, Central illumination only.  
> Graphics: The yellow/blue/orange analysis lines should look like neon markings floating in 3D space.  
> Flat Edges on ground are slightly glowing (Deep Blue).  
> 
> **4. OUTPUT QUALITY:**  
> Render in 8k resolution, highly detailed textures, sharp focus, professional architectural presentation.
