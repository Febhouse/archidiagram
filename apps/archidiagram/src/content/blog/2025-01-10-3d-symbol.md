The 3D symbol file is a great resource to help you quickly build architectural diagrams, planning analysis diagrams, and site mapping right inside SketchUp.

## What Can You Do with These Files?

If you prefer to work with the raw files instead of the new plugin, this download still offers a lot of flexibility:

1. **Software Compatibility:**
   - The 2D files can be opened in Photoshop (includes motion setup).
   - The 3D files open natively in SketchUp, but you can also export them into Rhino.
2. **Full Customization:**
   - Because they are 3D components, these symbols can be viewed and rotated from any angle in SketchUp, ensuring they always match your camera perspective.

## What's Included in the Download Folder?

When you download the package, you will get:

1. `SYMBOLS.skp` (The 3D symbols file, ready to open with SketchUp)
2. `SYMBOLMOTION.zip` (Files and assets for creating motion in Photoshop)
